    Now playing (Spotify)

    1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard on a computer, log in with your Spotify account (it must be Premium), and click Create app. Any name is fine.
    2. Under Redirect URIs paste exactly: then save.
    3. Copy the app's Client ID into Settings → Keys & links, and save.
    4. Press Connect Spotify below and approve. Your browser then jumps to a page that cannot load (that's expected). Copy that page's address from the address bar and paste it in the box below.

    Doing this on the Pi's own browser (or over ssh -L 8080:127.0.0.1:8080 pi@dashboard.local from a laptop) finishes by itself, no pasting needed.
